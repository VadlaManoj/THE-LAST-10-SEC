The rules of the loop

HOW TO PLAY

You have exactly 10 seconds. Every move you make is recorded. When the clock runs out, your past self returns to do it again.

1 Move around the room

Use WASD or the arrow keys. On mobile, use the on-screen directional pad.

2 Interact with switches

Get close to a glowing switch and press E. Switches stay on for the rest of that attempt.

3 Let time run out

Your position and interactions are recorded. The next attempt replays them as a translucent ghost.

4 Cooperate with yourself

Ghosts replay simultaneously. Use switches, pressure plates, and laser controls to reach the exit.
